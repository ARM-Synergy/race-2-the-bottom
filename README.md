## [2026 ARM Summer School](https://arm-development.github.io/arm-summer-school-2026/) Project 

Three-layer Neural Net with Long Short Term Memory designed to extend the Microwave Radiometers 
range of precipitable water vapor (PWV) detection. 

## Project Scope

- Water vapor strongly absorbs at wavelength of ~900 nm. High PWV reduces the downwelling direct beam irradiance near that frequency. Therefore we are expecting that the shortwave irradiance decreases with higher water vapor concentrations. 
- However shortwave (SW) irradiance is also affected by clouds and by Solar zenith angle
- A model that leverages concurrent microwave and solar radiometry to extend the radiometric retrievals (to the low end) where microwave is signal challenged.
